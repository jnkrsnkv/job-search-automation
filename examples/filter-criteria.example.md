# Filter criteria — EXAMPLE (fictional)

A sample for a made-up person: a backend developer looking for remote work in the
EU, wanting to move from an outsourcing shop to a product company.

---

## Hard red flags

- Company registered in <a sanctioned jurisdiction> or paying taxes there.
- Gambling, betting, casinos, sports wagering.
- Crypto projects with no real product (pure trading / "tokenomics").
- Staff-augmentation agencies renting out developers by the hour.
- Status not verifiable from open sources → don't exclude, write "not verified".

## Market red flags (into notes)

- "Looking for a rockstar / ninja / someone who goes the extra mile."
- The stack isn't named even in broad strokes.
- On-call with no extra pay, "occasionally on weekends."
- "First engineer" with no CTO and no funding round.

## Green flags

- A product company with its own SaaS.
- An engineering blog, a public changelog, conference talks.
- The stack is stated and close to mine (Go / Postgres / k8s).
- Async culture, a distributed team across 3+ time zones.

---

## Tracks and keywords

| Track | Priority | Keywords |
|---|---|---|
| Backend (Go) | 1 | golang developer, go engineer, backend engineer go, platform engineer |
| Backend (general) | 2 | backend engineer, software engineer backend, API engineer |
| Platform / Infra | 3 | platform engineer, infrastructure engineer, SRE, devops engineer |

Look at the content: "Full-stack" with a clear backend lean — fits. "Backend" that
turns out to be maintaining legacy PHP — doesn't fit.

**Doesn't fit by experience:** ML/data engineering (no experience), tech lead / EM
with 5+ direct reports, roles requiring Rust in production.

**Formal minimum requirements:** I don't have a CS degree.
- "CS degree required" → don't record.
- "CS degree or equivalent experience" / "nice to have" → record.

**Level:** willing to take a mid-level role for a strong product company. Senior-only
with a formal 7+ years requirement — don't record unless the company is in the
green zone.

---

## Location and work mode

- **Germany (my base)** — all modes.
- **EU / EEA** — remote only, or hybrid with travel no more than once a month.
- **UK / US** — full remote only, with an explicit "hire anywhere in EU".
- Mode not stated → "work mode not verified" in the notes, don't exclude.
- Language: working English required; German-only postings — don't record.

### Geographic skew

If the database fills with London hybrid roles — don't "search more in Berlin",
raise the weight of fully-remote-EU jobs.

---

## Green zone

**Category:** dev-tools and infrastructure companies with a strong engineering
brand.

**Explicit list:**
- <your dream company 1>
- <your dream company 2>

Waived: the location rule, the level rule. Always checked: hard red flags, a track
match, priority. A `[green zone]` tag in "Rationale".

## Outside the green zone — High only

Medium / Maybe outside the green zone — don't record, at most a line in the report.

---

## Priority

- **High** — track 1–2 + green-flag company + fresh + no red flags.
- **Medium** — track matches, weak green flag, or track 3.
- **Maybe** — tangential / unverified red flag / big gap.

First line of "Rationale":
- `High — Go backend at a product SaaS, engineering blog, remote EU confirmed`
- `Maybe — "backend" is actually maintaining a PHP monolith, not my stack`

---

## Freshness

- Compare the deadline / publication date against today.
- Passed deadline → don't record.
- Telegram `t.me/s/` — on suspiciously old dates, re-check with a repeat request.
